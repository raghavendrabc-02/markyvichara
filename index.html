About ನನ್ನ ಬಗ್ಗೆ

The storyteller behind Markyvichara.

I'm Raghu Chandrashekar, a Kannada content creator from Bengaluru. On Markyvichara I tell business, startup, marketing and tech stories in Kannada: why a brand wins, why a founder falls, how a campaign worked, and what every student and early entrepreneur in Karnataka can learn from it.

I'm a software engineer by profession and co-founded the clothing brand Rawnn, so I build things as well as talk about them.
